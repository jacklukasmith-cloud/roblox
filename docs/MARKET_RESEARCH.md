# What makes top Roblox simulators different

Research done October 2026. Revenue and player-count numbers online mostly come from third-party
trackers (rough estimates), so this focuses on **mechanics**, which are well documented.

| Game | What sets it apart | What Mythic Tamers borrows |
|---|---|---|
| **Pet Simulator 99** (BIG Games) | Layered progression (hatch → rebirth → new worlds), **Huge/Titanic** pets plus Gold/Rainbow variants, **weekly Clan Battles** with tiered prizes (whole top clan gets an exclusive pet), constant limited-time events, a trading economy, and **"Potato Mode"** for low-end phones | Shiny/Rainbow **variants**; **House War** (a lighter clan battle); Champion creatures only from the competitive track; **Low Graphics** toggle; server-wide shout-outs for rare hatches |
| **Grow a Garden** (BMWLux, later Splitting Point) | Shops **restock on a timer** (seeds/gear about 5 min, eggs about 10 min) and every server sees the same stock; **weather events** (Rain, Frost, Night, Meteor Shower) cause **mutations** worth more; developers trigger live **"admin abuse"** events; weekly updates; peaked around 22M concurrent players | **Wandering Merchant** with global 5-min restocks; **magical weather** synced across servers that boosts Shiny odds, luck or Essence; **admin live events** sent to every server; a paid "restock now" product (GAG sells the same thing) |
| **Bee Swarm Simulator** (Onett, 2018 to now) | Hidden depth (bee abilities, weather, gifted bees), NPC quests, and a long-term goal you discover over time | **Bestiary** with a permanent bonus per species. Quest NPCs are the next item on the roadmap |
| **Fisch** | A skill-based minigame, a **bestiary** of hundreds of fish, and mutations that change value | Bestiary completion as a long-term goal. A skill minigame is a future idea (see roadmap) |
| **Steal a Brainrot** | Idle income per second plus **PvP base stealing**, which drives constant social conflict | **Offline earnings**: creatures keep gathering while you're away (a reason to come back). Stealing was skipped on purpose: it's hard to make fair for younger players |
| **Adopt Me!** | A deep trading economy and social roleplay | Not yet. Trading needs anti-scam UI and a trade-lock window. Next big feature |

## Patterns shared by the winners
1. **Several overlapping timers** (restock, weather, events) keep giving players "one more minute" reasons to stay.
2. **Scarcity you can see**: limited stock, rare variants, exclusive event rewards.
3. **Things happening at the same time on every server** feel like real events, so players talk about them.
4. **Social competition**: clans or houses, leaderboards, server announcements.
5. **Constant live updates.** Grow a Garden's developers moved to weekly updates with dedicated event teams.
6. **Monetize convenience and speed, not winning.** Passes like 2x currency or auto-collect plus repeat purchases (potions, restocks).
